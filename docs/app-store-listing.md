## Description (4000 max)

ReadScape is a quiet, beautiful journal for the books in your life.

Keep track of what you're reading, remember how each book made you feel, and hold on to the lines you never want to forget.

SAY HOW IT FEELS
Tap the mic and just talk. Read a line aloud, or say what a chapter did to you. ReadScape turns it into a mood, a saved quote and a note in your own words, and keeps the recording so you can hear it again later.

YOUR LIBRARY
Add books in seconds from a catalogue of millions. Choose the cover edition you actually own. Sort by reading, finished, waiting or put down.

HOW IT FELT
Log moods as you read: loving it, hooked, tense, moved or a slow read. See at a glance how each book felt.

QUOTES AND NOTES
Save passages with page numbers and write down your thoughts. Share a favourite quote as a beautiful card.

PHOTOS
Keep pictures of your reading spots, your shelves and the books themselves.

INSIGHTS
See how long each book kept you, how your reading felt, your streak and your year in books with a yearly wrap.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. Your voice notes are stored privately, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time from inside the app.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers

Tracking: **No**. The app does not track users.

Data collected, all **linked to the user**, **not used for tracking**, purpose **App Functionality**:

| Apple's category | What it is in ReadScape |
|---|---|
| Contact Info → Name | Account name |
| Contact Info → Email Address | Account email |
| User Content → Photos or Videos | Book and reading-spot photos |
| User Content → Audio Data | Voice-note recordings |
| User Content → Other User Content | Books, ratings, moods, quotes, notes, reading goal, bio |
| Identifiers → User ID | Account ID |

Not collected: location, contacts, health, financial info, browsing history, search history (book searches go to Open Library and aren't stored), diagnostics, usage data.
