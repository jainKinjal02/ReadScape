## Promotional text (170 max, can change any time without review)

## Description (4000 max)

Readscape is a quiet, beautiful journal for the books in your life.

Track what you're reading, remember how each book made you feel, and keep the lines you never want to forget.

BRING YOUR GOODREADS SHELVES
Sign in to Goodreads once and Readscape brings over your shelves, ratings, read dates and reviews. Import again any time and changes you made on Goodreads come across too.

SAY HOW IT FEELS
Tap the mic and just talk. Read a line aloud, or say what a chapter did to you. Readscape turns it into a mood, a saved quote and a note in your own words, and keeps the recording so you can hear it again.

YOUR LIBRARY
Add books in seconds from a catalogue of millions, with the cover of the edition you own. Keep them as reading, read, waiting or put down, and rate them in halves.

HOW IT FELT
Log moods as you read: loving it, hooked, tense, moved or a slow read, and see at a glance how each book felt.

QUOTES AND NOTES
Save passages with page numbers, write down your thoughts, and share a favourite quote as a beautiful card.

PHOTOS
Keep pictures of your reading spots, your shelves and the books themselves.

INSIGHTS
See how long each book kept you, how your reading felt, your streak, and your year in books.

YOUR PROFILE
Your photo, a short bio, your pronouns, and links to your Instagram, TikTok, X and Goodreads.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. Your voice notes are stored privately, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time from inside the app.

**Notes** (paste into the Notes box):

> Readscape is a personal reading journal.
>
> Demo account above has books already added. Sign in with "Sign In" → email and password.
>
> Voice notes: open any book and tap "Say how it feels". The recording is transcribed and sorted into a mood, a quote and a note, which the user reviews before saving. The microphone is only used while recording.
>
> Goodreads import (profile menu → Import from Goodreads): Goodreads has no public API, so "Connect Goodreads" opens Goodreads' own sign-in page in an in-app browser. The user signs in on Goodreads' page (Readscape never sees the password), then the app uses Goodreads' own "Export Library" feature to download the user's library file and imports it. Nothing else is read, and nothing from the Goodreads session is kept. "Upload a file instead" imports an export the user downloaded themselves. A Goodreads account isn't needed to review the rest of the app.
>
> Account deletion: Home → tap the initials circle (top right) → "Delete account" at the bottom of the menu. For Sign in with Apple accounts this also revokes the Apple sign-in.
>
> Sign in with Apple is offered alongside Google sign-in.
