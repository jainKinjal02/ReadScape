# ReadScape Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

ReadScape is a personal reading journal. This policy explains what the app stores, why, who helps us run it, and how you can delete everything.

**The short version:** we store what you put into your journal so it's there when you come back. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

## What we store

**Your account**
- Your email address and name.
- Your password, if you create one. It is stored only as a secure hash by our database provider, and we never see it.
- If you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email address they share with us. With Apple, you can choose to hide your email; we then receive a private relay address instead.

**Your reading journal**
- The books you add, your reading status, the page you're on, your ratings and when you started and finished.
- Your reading goal, favourite genres and profile bio.
- The moods you log, and the quotes and notes you save.

**Photos**
- Photos you choose to add from your camera or photo library. Only the photos you pick are uploaded; ReadScape never reads the rest of your library.
- Photos are stored at unlisted web links. The links aren't shared or published anywhere, but anyone who had a photo's exact link could open it.

**Voice notes**
- When you record a voice note, the recording is uploaded to private storage that only your account can access.
- It is transcribed into text, and that text is sorted into a mood, a quote and a note, which you review before anything is saved.
- The recording is kept so you can play it back, until you delete the note or your account. If you discard a voice note before saving it, the recording is deleted straight away.
- ReadScape only uses the microphone while you are recording a voice note.

## Who helps us run ReadScape

- **Supabase** hosts our database, file storage and sign-in. Everything listed above is stored with Supabase.
- **OpenAI** transcribes voice-note recordings into text.
- **Anthropic** turns a voice note's text into a mood, a quote and a note.
- **Open Library** (Internet Archive) provides book search and cover images. When you search, your search words are sent to Open Library. Nothing that identifies you is sent.
- **Apple** and **Google** handle sign-in if you choose to use them.

OpenAI and Anthropic receive voice-note content only to process that one note. Under their API terms, they do not use it to train their models.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, and no ad or analytics tools in the app.
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
- No selling or renting your information to anyone.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete individual notes, quotes, photos and books at any time in the app.

To delete everything, open the profile panel on the Home screen and tap **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, your whole library, your notes, quotes and mood logs, your photos and your voice recordings. It can't be undone.

## Children

ReadScape is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
